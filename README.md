## Overview

The **Awareness Theory of Everything (ATE)** is a foundational framework proposing that **awareness — not matter or spacetime — is the primitive constituent of reality**.

From a single recursive principle involving identity, classification, complement-preserving negation, and closure, ATE aims to explain the emergence of:

- Time and space  
- Mass, particles, and forces  
- Quantum behavior and entanglement  
- Chemistry, thermodynamics, and stability  
- Complex systems, intelligence, and agency  

ATE is not a reinterpretation of existing physics, but a **structural substrate** from which known physical laws arise as *emergent constraints*.

---

## Core Idea (Informal)

At the most primitive level, reality consists of **identities** (Ids) capable of making distinctions.

When identities recursively classify:
- other identities,
- their own complements,
- and their own classifications,

**stable closed structures emerge**.

These closed recursive structures correspond to what physics calls:
- particles,
- fields,
- interactions,
- and conserved quantities.

Nothing external is added — complexity arises entirely from **recursive closure and stability conditions**.

## Relationship to Existing Physics

ATE does **not** discard established physics.

Instead, it:
- Reproduces known structures as emergent phenomena
- Provides structural explanations for empirical constants
- Reinterprets particles and forces as recursive stability classes
- Treats equations as awareness-contained objects rather than external laws

The framework engages with:
- Quantum mechanics
- Field theory
- Information theory
- Foundations of mathematics
- Philosophy of physics
